<Block id="current-problem" toc="Problem" kicker="Current Problem" title="A Simple Grid, A Weakened Brand Story">
The existing Starbucks landing page relied on a repetitive, two-column grid structure, limiting the depth of the brand experience.

<Figure slot="media" src="starbucks/current-landing.png" />
</Block>

<Block id="research" toc="Research" kicker="Market Research" title="How Seasonal Identity Is Expressed Beyond the Screen" layout="side">
Starbucks’ seasonal identity extends beyond digital interfaces into products, packaging, spaces, and in-store experiences. This research examined how seasonal identity is expressed across online and offline touchpoints.

<Figure slot="media" src="starbucks/composite-market.png" caption="Field Visual References" width={400} />
</Block>

<Block kicker="Competitor Research" title="How Competitors Communicate Brand and Seasonality">
This analysis compares how major coffee brands communicate brand values, product focus, and seasonality through their landing pages, identifying strengths, limitations, and opportunities for differentiation.

<Row slot="media" items={['starbucks/competitor-starbucks.png', 'starbucks/competitor-blue-bottle.png', 'starbucks/competitor-dunkin.png', 'starbucks/competitor-mccafe.png']} caption="Landing Page Comparisons" />
</Block>

<Block kicker="Design Goal" title="From Metric Insights to a Balanced, Personalized Design Direction">
I defined the design direction by balancing brand values and marketing elements, while enabling personalized seasonal storytelling beyond campaign-driven content.

<Row slot="media" items={[{ src: 'starbucks/design-direction-area.png', caption: 'New Design Area' }, { src: 'starbucks/composite-visual-design-direction.png', caption: 'Visual Design Direction' }]} />

<Block id="solution" toc="Solution" kicker="Solution" title="From an Information-Driven Structure to an Experience-Led Flow">
The redesigned experience begins with seasonal emotion and guides users through brand storytelling. I restructured the page to connect seasonality, brand philosophy, and user experience into a cohesive flow.

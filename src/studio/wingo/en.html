Overview

It had every feature, but it felt heavy and nobody remembered it.

WITIM is a collaboration platform for teams of 5 to 30 that brings chat, projects, calendar, tasks, files, attendance and notifications into one screen. The goal of this renewal was to turn the impression of a "heavy all-in-one" into "a flow that connects lightly." I put a winged character, Wingo (Wing + Go), at the center and tied everything from the name to the color, brand film and touchpoints into one story.

PROBLEM 01

Work is scattered across tools

Conversations, tasks, schedules and files live in different tools, so the team's flow keeps breaking.

Wingo lifts the scattered work at once and gathers it into one flow.

PROBLEM 02

All-in-one looks heavy

Every competitor says "all-in-one," and the more features there are, the more there seems to learn.

The light, quick feel of a wing says "an all-in-one that isn't heavy."

PROBLEM 03

No face to remember

With no memorable personality, there was nothing to extend into motion, video, social or merchandise.

A character that moves with a flap of its wing becomes the anchor for every extension.

Brand Essence

A wing that lightly connects a team's scattered work

WITIM is a team workspace that gathers conversations, tasks, schedules, files, attendance and notifications scattered across tools into one flow.

Its essence isn't "more features." It's the experience of lightly lifting scattered work so the team moves in the same direction. Wingo's wing stands for that: instead of piling up complex work, it organizes it into one flow and moves it forward.

Brand Slogan

Work lighter,
faster

WITIM cuts the time teams waste moving between tools and lets them see and act on what they need in one place.

The slogan puts WITIM's core value in the plainest words: connect scattered work so the team moves lighter, and speed up both decisions and execution. The brand film ends on the same line, so the brand sounds like one voice wherever you meet it.

Wingo Name Origin

Wing + Go,
a name that promises motion

Wingo combines Wing and Go.

Wing is the wing that lifts scattered work at once and gathers it into one flow. Go is the mobility that moves a stalled team to the next step, fast. The name itself carries the brand promise: move lighter, move faster.

Identity System

A wing you can still read at 16px

I added a single cyan stroke to the T of the WITIM wordmark and set the Wingo symbol in black and white. I scaled the app icon from 1024px down to 16px to check that the wing and the eye still read at small sizes, and split it into four variants for different backgrounds: light, primary blue, dark and monochrome.

Color

One cyan, and neutrals that carry its tone

I reduced WITIM's impression to three colors: an ink with a teal cast, a signal cyan as bright as a light, and a paper in the same tone. I re-derived the neutral scale from the cyan undertone of Primary #26D5FF (about 192°) so the three read as one family. Text on Signal is always set in ink #0B1416 for contrast.

Digital Touchpoints

Wingo greets you on every screen you open each day.

A collaboration tool gets opened dozens of times a day. I placed Wingo where people actually meet it, on the home screen, in a browser tab, in the desktop dock and in the product dashboard, to check that it keeps the same impression as size and background change.

Brand Experience

The same wing, off the screen

I extended the brand to things people can hold: a brand report, merchandise, a welcome kit for new hires and a subway billboard. To keep one character telling the same story at every touchpoint, each piece uses only the wing symbol and a single cyan.

Retrospective

Not selected, but carried all the way through

The Wingo proposal wasn't chosen as the final direction in the in-house competitive pitch. Still, it was a project where I took a single metaphor, wing = lightness = flow, through the name, character, color, brand film and touchpoints on my own, end to end.

01 · ONE METAPHOR

One story decides everything

I made the meaning of the name, the character's shape, the single cyan and the film's scenes all come from the same sentence. Whenever a decision stalled, going back to "does this feel as light as a wing?" gave the answer.

02 · AI AS A TOOL

Make fast with AI, set the criteria first

I made the film with Seedance and Runway and the mockups with ChatGPT. Because AI makes it easy to produce many options quickly, I first set an evaluation sheet weighted toward motion potential and how well each option explains the brand, then chose by it.
